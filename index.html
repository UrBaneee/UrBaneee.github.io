I build AI systems and measure whether they actually work.

Lately that means MAEDA, a data analyst made of several AI agents; a RAG framework built from scratch; and Evergreen, a system that keeps company knowledge up to date. Each one has its own evaluation before I trust the result.

I also make small tools I use every day, like LexiLearn for reading in English and jobmatch for job hunting.

I'm from Yangzhou. Since then I've taught in Thailand, studied in Nanjing, and spent time in Australia, Singapore and London before landing in the US.

Away from code, I'm at a piano or behind a film camera.
